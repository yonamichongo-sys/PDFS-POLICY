Privacy Policy

Offline PDF Viewer - All in One

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

Welcome to Offline PDF Viewer - All in One ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information, files, and device permissions.

1. 100% Offline Processing & File Privacy

Your documents and files are strictly private to you:

  • No Cloud Uploads: All PDF viewing, rendering, merging, splitting, document scanning, and text extraction operations are performed locally on your device.
  • No Document Storage on External Servers: We do not collect, read, upload, transmit, or store your documents, images, or files on any remote servers or cloud services.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user account registration, passwords, or personal identity information (such as your name, phone number, or physical address) to use the App.

  • Local App Data: Favorite bookmarks, recent document viewing histories, and reading settings (e.g., zoom preference, theme mode) are stored locally in the App's secure database on your device and never transmitted off your device.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The App requests only the permissions necessary to deliver core document reading and utility features:

  • Storage Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Storage Access Framework): Required exclusively to allow you to browse, search, and open PDF documents stored across your device's storage folders, and to save merged/split/scanned files to your chosen folder.
  • Camera Permission: Required solely if you choose to use the "Scan to PDF" tool to photograph physical receipts or paper documents and convert them into a local PDF. Photos taken in the scanner are processed on-device and never shared.
  • Internet Permission: Used solely to load non-intrusive advertisements served through Google AdMob. No document data is ever sent over this connection.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Services

To support the ongoing maintenance and development of this application, we use third-party advertising services:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers, device information, and diagnostic telemetry in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google manages data by visiting Google Advertising Policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal identification information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
